## 6. Data and infrastructure caveats that affect the reads

1. **Verdict-of-record files are stale for five studies.** `catalog.py` and `study-map.md` still print `NOT BUILT` or "no verdict" for `mechanical_baseline`, `selloff_put_diagonal`, `daily_vol_structure`, `structure_sweep` and `spy_benchmark`. They also carry the pre-re-price `ticker_pattern` flag. The dashboard renders from `catalog.py`, so it shows these wrongly. I used `current.md`. The memory index is also stale on `mechanical_baseline` ("study_review grading still owed") and `state_map` ("operator call owed").
2. **The seal and the peeks.** Two disclosed reads of sealed dates exist, on 2026-10-22 and 2026-11-21. The second was the bear-strategies peek. The second spent the forward reads of `index_bear_hedge`, `bear_arm` B2 and `hedge_timing` on 09-23 to 11-20. Never cite those figures. Any rule change from them needs a registration whose window starts after 2026-11-20. In that peek $19.1k of the bear plays' gain is open marks.
3. **The 2026 numbers are one open sell-off.** The window was picked after the market fell. A rebound would shrink every open mark. The sell-off peak (2026-10-06) is before the `index_bear_hedge` forward cutoff (2026-10-09).
4. **Two eras are never pooled, and they disagree.** v3 is frozen and is the evidence for the card. v4 is live, a bull tape, and `score_total` is not comparable. Several leads reversed between eras (`focus_list`, `adaptive_ladder`, GLD). The tier rules are derived on v3 and unvalidated on v4.
5. **Retro labels.** 93% of `state_map` Q5 labels and 251 of 301 `market_read` labels were written after the fact by a model that may know what came next.
6. **Tweak proxy rows carry 88% of the v4 loss** (PF 0.71 against 0.95 for real rows only). Real-only and real-plus-tweak figures answer different questions; quote both.
7. **Stale or missing caches.** `backtests/regime_watch/history.csv` is missing 19 sessions from 2026-10-21. The SMH OHLC cache stops 2026-11-13. `exit_mark_clock` and `narrow_to_fit` GN5 depend on unpriced rows.
8. **The deploy card may be stale.** The 2026-11-21 daily loop re-priced the backtest through 2026-11-20, but the journal step failed on Flex error 1003 ("Statement is not available", a Saturday), so the card was not rebuilt. The memory notes the card at session 2026-11-13 with analysis 5 days old on 2026-11-18.
9. **Unfetchable contracts.** 7 contracts for `mechanical_baseline` ARM RND and its overfitting check did not fetch; ARM U was never built, so ticker choice against a random name is untested. Never run two cache writers at once; the fetch guard self-matches `pgrep` (launch from a Python heredoc).
10. **`backtests/` is not disposable and has no git history.** Run `backup_research_caches.py push` once per fetch (about 530MB). Pull before any study on a fresh checkout.
11. **Registered-but-vacuous tables.** `state_map` TURN-ON prints `followed 0.000` by construction; a labelled post-hoc table sits beneath it and is never to be cited as registered.
12. **Small samples.** `selloff_put_diagonal` has 9 triggers. Each `signal_call_calendar` arm has about 20 calendars or fewer. `tail_breach` has 9 episodes, below its floor of 10. Several `NULL` words mean "no large effect", not "no effect".
