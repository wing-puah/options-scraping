Options flow research · as of 2026-10-21

Has the flow layer earned its keep?

An LLM reads Barchart options flow and proposes about ten plays a day. A ladder keeps the top one to three, the backtest prices them, and 48 pre-registered studies have tested what carries edge. Short answer: the vetoes and the exit rules do. The model's own skill at picking trades has not shown edge on v4, and the keep-or-kill call is still waiting on one forward read.

The evidence

Why the answer is "not yet" on v4

R is profit per trade in units of the risk taken. On v3 (the frozen evidence era, before costs) the tiers sort trades cleanly. On v4 (the current era, after commissions) most of the gap between them is gone. v4 is mostly a bull tape: 78% of its dates sit above SPY's 50-day average. So the ladder there acts mostly as a bet on the market going up.

What actually ships

These are the rules on the operator card, with the evidence behind each. They were derived on v3 and are not yet validated on v4.

Vetoes

Bear call spreads; any play in a model BEAR + high-vol regime; credit spreads in RANGE + low-vol; bear puts or long puts as a pick.

The strongest part of the ladder. Vetoed rows lost about $21k pooled. Bear: 0 of 496 subsets made money.
Cleared

Tiers A / B / C

A = bull call spread in RANGE or E-VOL. B = other bull call spreads, plus bull put spreads inside the delta and DTE band. C = everything else. Top 3 per day.

On v3, A vs C p = .0001, but A vs B was never separated (p = .98). On v4, the edge vanishes after costs.
Unvalidated on v4

Exits

Debit trades: take profit at 90%, stop at −75%, time exit at 75% of DTE, no trail. Credit bull puts: take profit at 65%, no stop.

exit_mechanism_study: every grid alternative lost to this profile. The be_after breakeven stop shipped and was reverted.
Cleared

BEAR_HE trail

Debit trades on mechanical BEAR + high or elevated vol: arm at +50%, then trail by 50 points.

Meets 5 of 6 criteria and adds about $4.4k in the study. It is a pre-gate exception, not a cleared rule.
Gated

Sizing

Tier A on a dip date (SPY below its 50-day average): normal size with a $1,000 stop. Everything else: $500 max risk. Skip a trade if one contract costs more than $500.

Tier A on dip dates: v4 +0.332, v3 +0.383. ruin_bound: P(ruin) is 0.0% at $500, against 17.8–25.8% for the alternatives. If dip_buy comes back null, sizing reverts to a flat $500.
In-sample policy

Hedge sleeve

Optional bear spread, at most half a position. No cheap far-OTM puts. Never open one because the day gapped up.

The sleeve loses money on balance. A gap-up trigger tested CONTRARY (−0.51 R).

What would change the picture

  • dip_buy forward read. This decides KEEP or RETIRE. It needs 30 dip days priced after 2026-09-23; 3 have happened so far, so about seven more months.
  • Other forward reads. adaptive_ladder, regime_map, refuse_floor_forward, index_bear_hedge and regime_confirm are still waiting. The last two need real sell-offs or BEAR flips after 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-21.
  • Ticker choice was never tested. ARM U ("random ticker from the day's flow") was never built. So nothing yet says whether the model picks good tickers.
  • Account size. The study is NOT FEASIBLE at $25k because drawdown reaches 35% against a 25% limit. The capital question is with the operator.
  • Next steps. P4 keeps the daily loop running at card sizes. Open trades are now re-priced daily until they finish. P5 re-runs the study suite once the sealed dates have priced.
